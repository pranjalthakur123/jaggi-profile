About

From Dharamshala's local leadership to public service

Devinder (Jaggi) is a Congress leader from Dharamshala, Kangra district, with a long association with public and local political life.

He has served in the Dharamshala Municipal Corporation, first as Deputy Mayor and later as Mayor. His experience in local governance has been an important part of his public service journey in Dharamshala.

He has also been associated with the Himachal Pradesh Congress Committee and was selected by the Indian National Congress as its candidate for the 2024 Dharamshala Assembly by-election.
